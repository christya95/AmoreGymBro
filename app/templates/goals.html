Daily starting targets for the guide’s {{ guide.target_lb }} lb target, not a food log.

Estimated daily maintenance (TDEE) uses Mifflin-St Jeor with your reported activity. The calorie guide subtracts 300 kcal/day. Protein uses 2 g per kg of saved weight; fat uses 0.8 g per kg, with the remaining calories allocated to carbs.
